# PRODUCT.md — ContractChill

## What it is
ContractChill is an AI-powered contract clarity tool. Upload a legal document (PDF, DOCX, TXT) and get an instant, plain-English breakdown: red-flag clauses, summaries, and negotiation tips, powered by Google Gemini. Bilingual (EN/ID).

## Who it is for
Freelancers, founders, and creators who sign legal documents without a lawyer on hand. They are smart but not legally trained; legal jargon is the enemy, and signing blind is the fear.

## What the visitor must do (landing page, Persuade mode)
Believe that ContractChill makes contracts understandable in seconds, then start free (→ /login).

## Product truth that must stay true
- Formats: PDF · DOCX (up to 10MB)
- 4 AI personas: Angry Lawyer, Chill Friend, Corporate Mentor, Freelancer Senior
- Average analysis under 15 seconds
- Bilingual EN/ID, powered by Google Gemini
- Documents are private and encrypted
- AI analysis is not legal advice (disclaimer in footer)
- Existing app copy lives in `client/src/i18n/locales/{en,id}.json` — do not paraphrase product claims in components.

## Voice
Plain, calm, specific. The brand name is playful ("chill") but the subject is serious (contracts). Warm and human, never corporate salesy, never legally intimidating.
