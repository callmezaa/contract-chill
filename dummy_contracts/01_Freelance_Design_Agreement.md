# INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT (GRAPHIC DESIGN SERVICES)

**Date:** May 19, 2026
**Client:** OmniCorp Global Solutions ("Client")
**Contractor:** Independent Designer ("Contractor")

## 1. SCOPE OF WORK
The Contractor agrees to provide graphic design services, including but not limited to branding, UI/UX design, and marketing materials as requested by the Client. The Client reserves the right to request unlimited revisions until the Client is 100% satisfied with the final deliverables. No additional fees shall apply for revisions.

## 2. TIMELINE AND DELIVERY
The Contractor must deliver all initial concepts within 24 hours of receiving a brief. Failure to meet this deadline will result in a 20% penalty deduction from the total project fee per day of delay.

## 3. COMPENSATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
The total compensation for the project is $1,500 USD. The Client will process the payment Net-90 (90 days) after the final approval of all deliverables and receipt of a valid invoice. If the Client's accounting department requires additional verification, payment may be delayed up to 120 days.

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Upon commencement of this agreement, all concepts, sketches, and final designs created by the Contractor—whether selected by the Client or rejected—shall immediately become the exclusive property of the Client. The Contractor waives all moral rights and may not use any of the work in their personal portfolio without written permission from the Client's legal department.

## 5. TERMINATION
The Client may terminate this agreement at any time, for any reason, without prior notice. In the event of termination by the Client before project completion, the Client is not obligated to pay for any incomplete work or hours billed up to the date of termination.
