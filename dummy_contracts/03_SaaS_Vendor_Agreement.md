# MASTER SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE (SAAS) AGREEMENT

**Date:** May 19, 2026
**Provider:** CloudScale Solutions Inc. ("Provider")
**Customer:** Enterprise Client ("Customer")

## 1. SUBSCRIPTION SERVICES
The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access the CloudScale platform. The initial term of this agreement is twelve (12) months. 

## 2. AUTO-RENEWAL AND FEES
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods unless the Customer provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the current term. The Provider reserves the right to increase subscription fees by up to 25% upon each renewal term without prior written consent from the Customer.

## 3. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
The Provider aims to maintain a 99.9% uptime. However, the Provider offers no credits, refunds, or penalties if the uptime falls below this threshold. Scheduled maintenance, emergency downtime, and third-party API failures are excluded from uptime calculations.

## 4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Provider’s total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total amount paid by the Customer in the one (1) month preceding the incident. The Customer assumes full liability for any data breaches occurring on their end and shall indemnify the Provider for any third-party claims.

## 5. DATA USAGE
The Provider reserves the right to use the Customer's anonymized data to train its proprietary machine learning models and may sell aggregated industry insights to third parties.
