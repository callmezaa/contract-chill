**Date:** May 19, 2026
**Brand:** TrendyFit Apparel ("Brand")
**Creator:** Influencer Name ("Creator")

## 1. DELIVERABLES
The Creator agrees to post two (2) Instagram Reels, three (3) TikTok videos, and five (5) Instagram Stories featuring the Brand's products over the next 30 days. The Creator must submit all content to the Brand for approval 7 days prior to posting. The Brand may request unlimited edits.

## 2. EXCLUSIVITY
For a period of twenty-four (24) months from the date of the last post, the Creator agrees not to promote, wear, or mention any competing fitness, lifestyle, or clothing brands on any of their social media channels, whether sponsored or organic. 

## 3. COMPENSATION
The Creator will receive a base payment of $500. An additional performance bonus of $1,000 will be paid ONLY IF the sponsored posts generate a combined total of 1,000,000 views and result in at least 500 direct sales using the Creator's promo code within 7 days of posting.

## 4. CONTENT OWNERSHIP (UGC)
The Creator grants the Brand a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use, modify, and distribute the Creator's name, likeness, and submitted content across all advertising channels (including billboards and paid ads) forever, without any additional compensation.

## 5. TERMINATION AND PENALTIES
If the Creator deletes any sponsored post before 12 months have passed, or fails to meet the engagement metrics outlined in Section 3, the Creator must refund the base payment in full and pay a penalty of $2,000 for breach of contract.
